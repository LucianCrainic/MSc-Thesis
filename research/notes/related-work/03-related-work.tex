\chapter{Related Work and Research Gap}
\label{chap:related-work}

This chapter first surveys CPU inference and tiered-memory research, then
examines the small body of evidence measured on or directly adjacent to
Xeon Max.  The two views are kept separate so that results from other
architectures inform mechanisms without being treated as measurements of
the CRESCO8 platform.
