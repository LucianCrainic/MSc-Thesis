\chapter{Theoretical and Technical Background}
\label{chap:theoretical-background}

This chapter introduces the concepts needed to follow the thesis.  It
begins with large language models, tokens and autoregressive inference,
then describes the inference pipeline and the engines that execute it.
The cluster overview provides the computing context.  The final
sections explain hardware performance limits through the Roofline
model and define the latency and throughput metrics used to evaluate
inference.
